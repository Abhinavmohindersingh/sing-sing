When something is different

What the dashboard does in the less common cases.

Shifts and staff
A worker is sickAnother worker is swapped onto the shift. The client’s invoice does not change.
A shift is cancelledIt stays on the roster, marked cancelled, and is not billed or paid.
One worker, 2 or 3 clientsOne group shift. Each client is billed, and the pay is split, as today.
A session at the Ilma CentreIt goes in the Ilma Centre row. A worker is optional.
RespiteBilled and paid at the day rate, not by the hour.
A worker is booked twiceA warning shows before the shift is saved.
A new worker, or one who leavesAdded once in the database. When someone leaves, their past shifts stay.
Hours differ from the rosterThe shift is edited to the hours worked, before invoices are made.
Invoices and claims
A public holidayHoliday rates and pay are used for that date.
No rate for a service on that dayThe shift and its invoice are marked “Check needed”.
New NDIS prices on 1 JulyNew rates start from that date. Older shifts keep the old rate.
A shift changes after the invoice was sentThe invoice is marked to be sent again.
A client has a plan managerTheir invoice goes to the plan manager’s email.
A client’s NDIS plan has endedA warning shows before the shift is billed.
The NDIS rejects a claim lineThe line is marked, fixed, and claimed again.
An email does not arrive (Type 2)It is marked on the Email page, to be sent again.
