The idea in three parts

For members

Join with a mobile number and a membership card, then get content, Stories and match day in one place.

A direct line

Members can send questions to players, and the club can reach every member, a town, or one person.

For the club

A simple site to run it day to day, and for the first time a clear view of its fans in PNG.
