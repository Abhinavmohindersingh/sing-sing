Structure

Website Map

A home page that introduces TES, a page for each range, a page of its own for every crane model, and the service behind them. Easy to find your way around, and easy for Google to read.

Built to grow
STRUCTURE
New models and services drop into the same structure later, without a rebuild.

Why every machine gets its own page: Google ranks pages, not whole sites. Give each crane and hoist a page of its own and TES can turn up when someone searches a specific model, not just "tower cranes". They all come off one template, so the range can grow later without new design work.

Initial design

What it looks like

An early look at the direction. A few screenshots to show the theme and the building blocks the site is made from. Nothing is locked in yet, and the photos are just placeholders for now.

Hero image
One machine on the TES navy, a line of positioning and two actions. Can run as video instead of a still.
Six machines, one column
Rivals leave these figures in a PDF. We lift them onto the page so the range compares down one column. The maker sheet still sits on each machine as a download.
The load curve
A real graph per machine, not a picture of one. Hover it and it reads capacity at any radius.
Everything TES offers
Cranes, batteries, hoists and platforms, plus repairs, parts and monitoring. Each gets a page of its own.
The design language is TES's own. Palette, type and tone come straight from the battery deck, terms and calculator, so the website and those documents read as one company rather than two.
