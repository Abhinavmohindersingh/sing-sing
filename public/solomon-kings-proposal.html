The plan

Project architecture: four sequential gated stages

Each stage must fully work and be verified before the next stage starts. Writing the lessons starts in Stage 2 and never really stops.

Stage 1
Working prototype
$13,500
  • Prototype foundation: the front end, built once and reused
  • Hosting and auth: online, with secure user logins
  • Small LLM: a lightweight AI model in the background, for demos
  • Sample content: a very short set of lessons
Done whenAnyone can open the prototype, pick any age group, finish a short lesson and talk to Soli.

Stage 2
Content and AI foundation
$73,750
  • Content framework: our own lesson structure
  • Data pipeline: collection, labelling and storage
  • Translation model: Pijin and English, trained and checked
  • AI training: first batches, run and tested in the background
  • Compliance: child-safety and data-protection checks
Done whenEnough content is in, and the AI is capturing results.
3
Stage 3
Pilot and improvement
$36,500
  • Live sessions: the first real classes with children
  • Feedback loop: gather it, fix what breaks
  • RLHF: real users rate the answers, and that feedback trains the AI
  • Retraining: on real session data
Done whenThe data shows children learning, and the app holds up in real use.

Renting or owning

Four H100s rented and always on cost about $14,700 a month. A 4-GPU server costs about $225,000 plus about $3,150 a month for space and power, so buying pays back in about 19 months, and only if the machines are busy all the time.

So

Rent while use is small or changing. Buy only when the load is steady and known.

Risk

Risks, and how we reduce them

What could go wrong, in plain words, and what we do about it.
What could go wrongHow we reduce it
Children stop using it
Short football lessons, tried with real users before launch
The AI gives a wrong or unsafe answer
Child-safety checks, and real users rate the answers
Pijin or English is wrong
Local checks, and a translation model trained on real content
Running costs grow with use
Costs follow use. Start hosted, rent or buy later
Children’s data is exposed
Data-protection checks, and child data kept in our own database
